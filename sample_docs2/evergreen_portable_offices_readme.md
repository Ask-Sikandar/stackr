# Evergreen Portable Offices - Sample Knowledge Base README

This sample document represents a different business domain from container sales. It is written in a similar style and can be used as seed content for retrieval, QA, and assistant evaluation.

## Business Overview

Evergreen Portable Offices sells and leases temporary office buildings for construction sites, schools, municipal projects, healthcare overflow, and commercial relocations.

- Headquarters: Denver, CO
- Service area: 32 US states
- Support hours: Mon-Fri, 7:00 AM-6:00 PM MT
- Emergency dispatch: 24/7 for active lease customers

## Core Products

### Mobile Office Trailers

Single-wide and double-wide office trailers delivered turnkey.

- Typical sizes: 8x20, 10x40, 12x60
- Capacity: 2-24 staff depending on layout
- Common use: Jobsite PM offices, temporary admin space

### Modular Classroom Units

ADA-compliant modular classrooms for K-12 districts and private campuses.

- Optional smartboard wiring and acoustic paneling
- Fire/life safety package available by district code
- Typical lead time: 3-6 weeks after permit approval

### Medical Intake Pods

Rapid-deploy intake and triage units for hospitals and clinics.

- Isolated zones for registration and nurse screening
- HEPA-ready HVAC options
- Priority build queue for healthcare projects

## Delivery and Logistics

### Standard Lead Times

- In-stock office trailers: 5-10 business days
- Custom interior layout: 10-20 business days
- Classroom and medical pods: 15-30 business days

### Site Requirements

- Clear access for 53 ft truck and trailer
- Level pad with compacted gravel or concrete
- Utility stubs marked before delivery day
- Overhead clearance free of wires/branches

### Delivery Window Policy

Customers receive a 4-hour delivery window and a driver call 45 minutes before arrival.

- First failed delivery due to inaccessible site: $350 re-delivery fee
- Weather delays: No penalty; rescheduled at next available slot

## Warranty and Service

### Lease Coverage

All active leases include:

- Structural warranty for full lease term
- HVAC and electrical service labor
- Next-business-day dispatch in metro areas

### Purchase Coverage

- 3-year structural warranty
- 1-year mechanical/electrical warranty
- Extended coverage available up to 7 years

## Payment Terms

- Lease: First month + setup fee due before dispatch
- Purchase: 25% deposit, balance due prior to final placement
- Accepted methods: ACH, wire, corporate card (2.5% processing)
- Net-30 invoicing available for approved municipal/school accounts

## Cancellation and Refunds

- Cancel 10+ business days before delivery: Full refund of deposit
- Cancel 5-9 business days before delivery: 50% deposit refund
- Cancel fewer than 5 business days: Deposit non-refundable
- Custom-built units are non-refundable once fabrication begins

## Frequently Asked Questions (Condensed)

### Can you deliver without a crane?

Yes. Most single-wide units are delivered with tilt trailer systems. Double-wide installations may require crane placement depending on site constraints.

### Do you handle permits?

We provide permit drawing sets and engineering stamps where required. Permit filing responsibility depends on contract type and local jurisdiction.

### Can we buy out a lease unit later?

Yes. Most lease agreements include a buyout schedule after month 12.

### Is internet included?

No. We provide conduit and network-ready wiring; customer or IT vendor provisions service.
