# Delivery Policy

## Coverage Area

Pacific Container Co. delivers to all 48 contiguous United States. We do not currently deliver to Hawaii or Alaska, but can arrange third-party shipping for an additional fee — contact us for a custom quote.

## Texas Delivery

We maintain a depot in Dallas, TX and deliver throughout Texas including Houston, San Antonio, Austin, Fort Worth, El Paso, and all rural areas. Standard lead time for Texas deliveries is 3–5 business days.

Rush delivery (1–2 business days) is available in select markets for an additional $350. Contact us to check availability.

## Delivery Requirements

- A flat, firm surface is required for placement (gravel, asphalt, concrete, or compacted soil).
- Clearance of at least 14ft height and 10ft width is required for our tilt-bed trucks.
- Customer must be present at time of delivery.
- If access is restricted, customer is responsible for arranging final-mile placement.

## Container Placement

Our drivers use tilt-bed trucks for safe ground-level placement. We do not use cranes. If you require crane placement (e.g., rooftop or elevated installation), you will need to arrange that separately.
