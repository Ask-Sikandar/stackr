## When to Choose One-Trip

One-trip containers are ideal when aesthetics matter — home conversions, retail pop-ups, food storage, or any application where the container will be visible. The near-perfect surface takes paint and modifications beautifully.

## When to Choose Grade A Used

Grade A used containers offer the best value for most commercial and industrial applications: job site storage, equipment housing, portable offices, and long-term outdoor storage. Minor surface rust does not affect structural integrity or waterproofing.

## When to Choose Grade B Used

Grade B containers are the budget choice for temporary construction storage, agricultural use, or applications where looks don't matter. Every unit is WWT-certified before it leaves our yard. If you're storing materials for 6–18 months on a tight budget, Grade B is a solid choice.

We provide a condition inspection report for every unit. One-trip containers include a container tracking record showing their import voyage. Ask your sales rep for a pre-purchase inspection report.
