# Container Specifications

## 10ft Mini Container

The 10ft mini container is ideal for small storage needs and tight spaces.

- External dimensions: 10ft × 8ft × 8.5ft (L × W × H)
- Internal dimensions: 9ft 4in × 7ft 8in × 7ft 10in
- Tare weight: 2,900 lbs
- Max cargo weight: 22,000 lbs
- Capacity: 583 cubic feet
- Door opening: 7ft 8in × 7ft 6in

## 20ft Standard Container

The 20ft standard container is our most popular general-purpose unit.

- External dimensions: 20ft × 8ft × 8.5ft (L × W × H)
- Internal dimensions: 19ft 4in × 7ft 8in × 7ft 10in
- Tare weight: 4,850 lbs
- Max cargo weight: 47,900 lbs
- Capacity: 1,172 cubic feet
- Door opening: 7ft 8in × 7ft 6in
- Floor: 28mm hardwood marine-grade plywood
- Walls: 14-gauge corrugated Corten steel
- Roof: 2mm corrugated Corten steel

## 40ft Standard Container

The 40ft standard container doubles storage capacity over the 20ft at a lower per-cubic-foot cost.

- External dimensions: 40ft × 8ft × 8.5ft (L × W × H)
- Internal dimensions: 39ft 5in × 7ft 8in × 7ft 10in
- Tare weight: 8,160 lbs
- Max cargo weight: 59,040 lbs
- Capacity: 2,385 cubic feet
- Door opening: 7ft 8in × 7ft 6in

## 40ft High Cube Container

The 40ft High Cube adds an extra foot of vertical clearance — ideal for tall equipment, vehicles, or living conversions.

- External dimensions: 40ft × 8ft × 9.5ft (L × W × H)
- Internal dimensions: 39ft 5in × 7ft 8in × 8ft 10in
- Tare weight: 8,800 lbs
- Max cargo weight: 58,400 lbs
- Capacity: 2,660 cubic feet
- Door opening: 7ft 8in × 8ft 5in

## Construction Standards

All Pacific Container Co. units are constructed to ISO 1496-1 standards and CSC certified for international shipping. Steel grade is ASTM A-242 (Corten) for superior corrosion resistance.
