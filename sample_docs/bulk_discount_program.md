# Bulk Discount Program

Pacific Container Co. rewards volume buyers with automatic tiered discounts applied at checkout.

## Discount Tiers

| Units Ordered | Discount | Additional Perks |
|--------------|----------|-----------------|
| 1 | 0% | Standard delivery terms |
| 2–4 | 5% | Priority scheduling |
| 5–9 | 10% | Free delivery (up to 150 miles) |
| 10–19 | 15% | Free delivery (up to 300 miles) + dedicated account manager |
| 20+ | 20% | Free delivery nationwide + custom paint included + priority production slot |

Discounts apply to the container purchase price only, not to customization add-ons.

## Mixed Orders

Discounts apply to mixed-size orders. A customer ordering 3× 20ft and 2× 40ft qualifies for the 5-unit (10%) discount tier.

## Project Pricing

For large construction projects or recurring orders (e.g., modular buildings, pop-up retail), contact our commercial sales team for a project pricing agreement. Project agreements can lock in pricing for up to 12 months.

## Fleet Programs

Companies that purchase 5+ containers per year qualify for our Fleet Program:
- Dedicated sales rep
- Net-30 payment terms (subject to credit approval)
- Preferred delivery scheduling
- Volume rebate at year-end (up to 5% additional)

## How to Claim

Bulk discounts are applied automatically when you add units to your quote. For project and fleet pricing, contact commercial@pacificcontainer.com or call 1-800-555-0190.
