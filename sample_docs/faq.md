# Frequently Asked Questions

## Pricing & Payment

**How much does a 40ft container cost?**
A 40ft standard container starts at $2,800 for Grade B used, $3,850 for Grade A used, and $5,900 for new (one-trip). Pricing depends on condition grade and any customizations. Get an instant quote on our website or call 1-800-555-0190.

**Do you offer financing?**
We partner with EquipmentFinance.com for container financing. Terms from 12–60 months, rates from 6.9% APR (subject to credit approval). Ask your sales rep for a financing application.

**Can I get a price match?**
Yes. We match any written quote for an identical grade/size container within our delivery region. Send us the competitor quote and we will beat or match it.

## Delivery & Logistics

**How long does delivery take?**
Standard lead times range from 3–8 business days depending on your location. Rush delivery (1–2 days) is available in select markets for $350 extra.

**Do you deliver to Texas?**
Yes. We have a depot in Dallas and deliver throughout Texas. Standard lead time is 3–5 business days. Delivery fees start at $150 within 50 miles of our depot.

**What kind of truck do you use?**
We use tilt-bed (roll-off) trucks that deliver containers directly to ground level. No crane is needed for standard placement.

**What surface do I need for the container?**
A flat, firm surface — concrete, asphalt, gravel, or compacted soil. The surface must support up to 60,000 lbs.

## Container Condition

**What is a "one-trip" container?**
A one-trip container has been used exactly once to ship cargo from a manufacturer in Asia to a US port. It is essentially new — minimal surface marks, no rust, like-new doors and seals.

**What is the difference between Grade A and Grade B?**
Grade A containers are 5–10 years old, structurally sound, with minor surface rust and wear. Grade B containers are older (10–15 years), functional, and watertight but show visible wear, dents, and rust. Both are inspected and certified before sale.

**Are your containers wind and watertight?**
Yes. All containers we sell are inspected and certified wind and watertight (WWT) before delivery. We provide a WWT certificate with every unit.

## Modifications & Customization

**Can I add a door in the side of the container?**
Yes. We can cut and install a personnel door (36in × 80in) for $650. Larger barn-door openings are also available for $1,200.

**Can you insulate a container?**
Yes. We offer spray foam (2in or 4in), rigid board, and full interior liner packages. A 40ft container with 2in spray foam insulation (R-13) costs $1,800 for the insulation package.

**Do you install electricity?**
We offer electrical rough-in packages (wiring, panel, outlets, lights). A licensed electrician in your jurisdiction must inspect and certify the final installation.

## Warranty & Returns

**What warranty do you offer?**
We provide a 5-year structural warranty and a 1-year surface warranty on all units. One-trip containers include a 2-year surface warranty.

**Can I return a container?**
Containers can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they do not meet the agreed-upon condition grade. Return shipping is the customer's responsibility. Custom-modified containers are non-returnable.
