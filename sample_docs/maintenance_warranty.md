## Recommended Maintenance Schedule

### Annual (Every 12 Months)
- Inspect roof seams and apply roof sealant to any cracks or gaps
- Check door gaskets and lubricate hinges with WD-40 or similar
- Inspect floor for soft spots or damage; treat with penetrating wood sealer if needed
- Touch up any bare metal spots with Rustoleum or equivalent to prevent rust spread

### Every 3 Years
- Full exterior inspection by a qualified inspector
- Re-seal all seams and penetrations
- Consider repainting if surface rust is progressing

## Common Maintenance Tips

**Surface rust:** Light surface rust is normal and does not indicate a structural problem. Wire-brush the area, apply a rust converter, and top-coat with metal paint rated for exterior use.

**Door adjustment:** Doors that are stiff or difficult to close are usually caused by settling. Adjust the door cam-lock pins using the adjusting cam mechanism on the locking bars.

**Condensation:** Containers can experience condensation ("container rain") from temperature swings. Adding ventilation (roof vents or louvered side vents) and insulation significantly reduces condensation.

**Leveling:** Containers should be level within 1 inch across their length. Use concrete blocks, railroad ties, or adjustable container jacks. Unlevel placement strains the frame and can cause door issues.
