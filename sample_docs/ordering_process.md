# How to Order

Ordering from Pacific Container Co. is straightforward. Here is the process from first contact to container delivery.

## Step 1 — Get a Quote

Use the online quote tool on our website, call 1-800-555-0190, or chat with our AI assistant. Provide your zip code, container size, grade preference, and any customizations you need. You will receive a written quote within minutes online or within 2 business hours by phone.

## Step 2 — Review and Accept Your Quote

Your written quote is valid for 7 days and includes itemized pricing for the container, customizations, delivery fee, and applicable taxes. Review the quote and confirm your delivery address and access requirements.

## Step 3 — Pay Your Deposit

A 20% deposit reserves your container and locks in your price. You can pay by credit card (2.9% surcharge), ACH bank transfer (no surcharge), or wire transfer. Your deposit is fully refundable if you cancel 7+ business days before delivery.

## Step 4 — Customization (if applicable)

If you ordered modifications (doors, windows, insulation, paint), your container enters our modification queue. Standard modifications take 3–7 business days. You will receive a production update email when modifications begin and when they are complete.

## Step 5 — Schedule Delivery

Once your container is ready, our logistics team will contact you to schedule delivery. We offer morning (8am–12pm) and afternoon (12pm–5pm) delivery windows. Same-day scheduling is occasionally available for in-stock, unmodified units.

## Step 6 — Delivery and Placement

Our driver will call 30 minutes before arrival. Please ensure the delivery site is accessible, clear of obstacles, and that a responsible adult is present to sign the delivery receipt. The driver will place the container using a tilt-bed truck.

## Step 7 — Final Payment

The remaining 80% balance is due before the delivery truck leaves your property. We accept the same payment methods as the deposit.

## After Delivery

You will receive your warranty certificate, WWT certification, and (for one-trip units) the container tracking record by email within 24 hours of delivery. If anything is not to the agreed standard, contact us immediately — we will make it right.
