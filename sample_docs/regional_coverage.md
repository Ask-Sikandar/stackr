# Regional Delivery Coverage

Pacific Container Co. operates from depots in Dallas TX, Atlanta GA, Chicago IL, Newark NJ, and Los Angeles CA. This gives us competitive delivery times across all 48 contiguous states.

## Depot Locations and Coverage

### Dallas, TX Depot
Primary service area: Texas, Oklahoma, Louisiana, Arkansas, New Mexico, Kansas
- Texas (all regions): 3–5 business days
- Oklahoma, Louisiana, Arkansas: 3–4 business days
- New Mexico, Kansas: 4–6 business days

### Atlanta, GA Depot
Primary service area: Georgia, Florida, Alabama, Mississippi, South Carolina, North Carolina, Tennessee
- Georgia, Alabama, Mississippi: 2–4 business days
- Florida (all regions): 3–5 business days
- Carolinas, Tennessee: 3–5 business days

### Chicago, IL Depot
Primary service area: Illinois, Indiana, Ohio, Michigan, Wisconsin, Minnesota, Iowa, Missouri
- Illinois, Indiana, Ohio: 2–4 business days
- Michigan, Wisconsin: 3–5 business days
- Minnesota, Iowa, Missouri: 3–5 business days

### Newark, NJ Depot

- New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania: 2–4 business days
- Connecticut, Massachusetts: 3–5 business days

### Los Angeles, CA Depot
Primary service area: California, Nevada, Arizona, Utah, Oregon, Washington
- Southern California: 2–4 business days

- Oregon, Washington: 4–6 business days
- Utah: 4–6 business days

## States with Extended Lead Times

The following states are served from the nearest depot but require additional transit time:
- Montana, Wyoming, Idaho, North Dakota, South Dakota: 7–10 business days
- Maine, Vermont, New Hampshire: 6–8 business days
- West Virginia, Kentucky: 4–6 business days (via Newark or Atlanta)

## Not Currently Served

- Hawaii: Third-party ocean freight available on request (~$2,500 additional)
- Alaska: Third-party freight available on request (~$3,500 additional)
- US Territories (Puerto Rico, Guam, etc.): Contact us for a custom quote
