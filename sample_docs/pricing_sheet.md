## Deposit & Payment

A 20% deposit is required to reserve a unit. Balance is due before delivery. We accept wire transfer, ACH, and major credit cards (2.9% surcharge on card payments).

## Refundable Deposit

Deposits are fully refundable if cancelled at least 7 business days before scheduled delivery. Cancellations within 7 days forfeit 50% of the deposit.

## Price Guarantee

We will match any written competitor quote for identical grade and size within our delivery region. Contact our sales team with the competing quote.
